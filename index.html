Affiliate Momentum

Affiliate Momentum is a private internal content publishing and analytics application used to create, schedule, and measure original content for an authorized Pinterest Business account. It is not offered as a public application and does not provide third-party access to Pinterest data.

Content publishing

Creates and schedules original Pins that link to relevant informational content.

Owned account analytics

Reads the authenticated account's own Pins and Boards to measure performance.

Private use

Used only by the account owner for internal business and creator workflows.
