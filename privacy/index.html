Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Road2FG Affiliate Momentum is a private internal content publishing and analytics application used by the account owner to manage an authorized Pinterest Business account.

Information accessed

When authorized through Pinterest, the application may access information associated with the authenticated account that is required for its stated functionality, including the account owner's Pins, Boards, and related performance or reporting data.

How information is used

The application uses authorized Pinterest data only to:

  • create and schedule original Pins;
  • select and manage the account owner's Boards;
  • measure content performance and engagement; and
  • improve future content decisions for the same authorized account.

Sharing and sale of data

The application is not offered to third-party users. Pinterest API data is not sold to advertisers or data brokers and is not shared with unrelated third parties.

Data retention and security

Only data reasonably necessary for publishing, attribution, reporting, and performance analysis is retained. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application credentials and stored operational data.

Authorization and deletion

Access is limited to accounts explicitly authorized by the account owner. Authorization can be revoked through the relevant Pinterest account or developer authorization controls. Stored application data may also be deleted when it is no longer needed for the internal workflow.

Children

The application is an internal business tool and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes or to reflect applicable platform requirements. Material changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions relating to Affiliate Momentum, contact the operator through the Road2FG GitHub account associated with this application.
